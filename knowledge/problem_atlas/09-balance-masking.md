# 09 Balance, masking and arrangement (201-225)

### 201 Vocal buried in the beat
- **severity:** major
- **says:** "can't hear the vocal", "vocal buried", "beat too loud"
- **cause:** level or masking.
- **check:** vocal vs beat level and band-by-band masking with fader compensation.
- **fix:** fix masking first (carve the beat), then raise the vocal a little.
- **evidence:** practice

### 202 Vocal too loud on top
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "vocal is too loud", "sticking out"
- **cause:** level too high or too bright.
- **check:** compare to a reference.
- **fix:** lower the vocal 1-2 dB; check top end.
- **evidence:** practice

### 203 Beat masks the vocal's presence
- **severity:** major
- **says:** "the vocal has no edge in the mix"
- **cause:** beat energy at 1-4 kHz.
- **check:** masking analysis.
- **fix:** dynamic EQ or Trackspacer on the beat keyed to the vocal.
- **evidence:** practice

### 204 Beat masks the vocal's body
- **severity:** major
- **says:** "the center sounds back", "center of the vocal sounds back", "vocal feels behind", "want it fuller and upfront without louder"
- **cause:** beat energy at 500-900 Hz where the voice's body sits.
- **check:** win rate vocal vs beat 630-800 Hz.
- **fix:** let the ducker act lower (Trackspacer low cut moved from about 1 kHz down to about 490 Hz).
- **evidence:** archive

### 205 Mastered two-track beat leaves no room
- **severity:** major
- **says:** "the beat is already loud", "no room for my vocal"
- **cause:** a limited beat with no headroom.
- **check:** beat loudness and crest factor (very loud, small crest).
- **fix:** turn the beat down 4-8 dB first; mid-channel dynamic EQ keyed to the vocal.
- **evidence:** practice, story:oldtownroad

### 206 Hi-hats too loud
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "hats are piercing", "too much hats"
- **cause:** hats loud in 6-12 kHz.
- **check:** hat level vs vocal top.
- **fix:** lower or shelf the hats.
- **evidence:** practice

### 207 Snare competes with words
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "snare covers the words"
- **cause:** snare transients at 2-5 kHz mask consonants.
- **check:** timing of snare vs vocal onsets.
- **fix:** a small dynamic dip on the snare keyed to the vocal.
- **evidence:** practice

### 208 Sample vocals clash with the lead
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the sample vocal is in the way"
- **cause:** a vocal sample occupies the lead's range.
- **check:** listen; masking analysis.
- **fix:** filter or duck the sample under the lead.
- **evidence:** practice

### 209 Hook not bigger than the verse
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the hook doesn't hit"
- **cause:** no arrangement or level lift.
- **check:** section loudness comparison.
- **fix:** add layers and width, small level lift, more space.
- **evidence:** practice

### 210 Ad-libs louder than the lead
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "ad-libs are too loud"
- **cause:** level.
- **check:** levels.
- **fix:** lower and darken ad-libs.
- **evidence:** practice

### 211 Vocal stack collapses in mono
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "stacks disappear in mono"
- **cause:** phase from widening.
- **check:** stereo compatibility.
- **fix:** use real takes panned.
- **evidence:** practice

### 212 Lead not centred
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the vocal leans left"
- **cause:** pan offset or a one-sided effect.
- **check:** pan value; stereo balance.
- **fix:** centre the lead.
- **evidence:** practice

### 213 Low end overwhelms the vocal
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "all bass, no vocal"
- **cause:** sub and 808 level too high.
- **check:** octave balance vs a reference in the genre.
- **fix:** lower the low end against the reference.
- **evidence:** practice

### 214 Too much happening at once
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "too busy", "cluttered"
- **cause:** arrangement density.
- **check:** count elements in the section.
- **fix:** mute parts; arrangement before EQ.
- **evidence:** practice

### 215 Pads wash out the vocal range
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the pad covers the vocal"
- **cause:** pads in 300 Hz-3 kHz.
- **check:** masking.
- **fix:** filter or duck the pad.
- **evidence:** practice

### 216 Guitar or piano compete at 200-500 Hz
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the keys make the vocal muddy"
- **cause:** shared low-mid range.
- **check:** masking.
- **fix:** cut the instrument there.
- **evidence:** practice

### 217 Mix sounds small and narrow
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "narrow", "small", "mono-ish"
- **cause:** everything panned centre.
- **check:** stereo width.
- **fix:** pan secondary elements, widen pads and ad-libs.
- **evidence:** practice

### 218 Mix too wide or phasey
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "phasey", "weird in mono"
- **cause:** too many wideners.
- **check:** correlation near or below 0.
- **fix:** remove wideners.
- **evidence:** practice

### 219 Left-right imbalance
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the mix leans to one side"
- **cause:** uneven panning.
- **check:** L/R level difference.
- **fix:** balance the panning.
- **evidence:** practice

### 220 Feature verse recorded differently
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the feature sounds different"
- **cause:** a different mic, room and chain.
- **check:** spectrum comparison with the main artist.
- **fix:** EQ-match; similar chain.
- **evidence:** practice

### 221 Intro or outro level jumps
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the intro is too quiet"
- **cause:** sections not balanced.
- **check:** loudness by section.
- **fix:** automation.
- **evidence:** archive

### 222 Ducking leaves holes in the beat
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the beat has holes when I sing"
- **cause:** a ducker set too deep.
- **check:** Trackspacer amount and range.
- **fix:** less amount, narrower range.
- **evidence:** practice

### 223 Beat feels lifeless under the vocal
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "the beat dies when I come in"
- **cause:** over-ducking or bus compression.
- **check:** beat level during vocal.
- **fix:** reduce ducking.
- **evidence:** practice

### 224 Some words buried
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "some words disappear"
- **cause:** no level rides.
- **check:** per-word levels.
- **fix:** volume automation on those words.
- **evidence:** practice

### 225 Balance shifts after the master limiter
- **severity:** minor
- **says:** "it sounded fine before the master chain"
- **cause:** mixing into a limiter changes the balance late.
- **check:** compare with master chain bypassed.
- **fix:** mix with light master processing; set balance first.
- **evidence:** practice
