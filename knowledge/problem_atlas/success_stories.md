# Success stories: hit records from unconventional setups

Each entry says only what the cited source supports, then what it teaches
for mixing and which atlas problems it relates to. If a technique isn't in
the source, it isn't here.

## Billie Eilish & Finneas: *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?* (2019)
- **Setup:** made in Finneas's small bedroom studio at their parents' house
  in Los Angeles. Monitoring: Yamaha HS5 + HS8S sub. Interface: a Roland
  Quad Capture early on, later a UA Apollo 8. "Ocean Eyes" was sung into an
  Audio-Technica AT2020, which Finneas calls a good starter mic; he later
  used a Neumann TLM 103. He records everyone on one mic, does many takes,
  and keeps working until a part is right
  ([Sound On Sound, Jul 2019](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish)).
  The album won Album of the Year and Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical
  ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2020/01/27/billie-eilish-grammy-2020-bedroom-studio-logic-pro-x/)).
- **Lesson:** a cheap condenser in an untreated bedroom is not the limit.
  Close, quiet, intimate delivery makes the room matter less, and take
  quality beats gear.
- **Atlas:** 002, 005, 038, 064.

## Bon Iver: *For Emma, Forever Ago* (2007)
- **Setup:** tracked in a hunting cabin in Wisconsin. All but a few vocal
  and horn tracks used a single Shure SM57 dynamic mic
  ([Lewis, *MUSICultures* case study](https://journals.lib.unb.ca/index.php/MC/article/download/20212/23314);
  [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/bon-iver-platinum-record-microphone)).
  Certified platinum.
- **Lesson:** a dark, mid-forward dynamic mic can be the record's
  character. Layering and arrangement carry it; don't EQ it into a
  condenser.
- **Atlas:** 039, 105, 157.

## Steve Lacy: *Steve Lacy's Demo* (2017), "Dark Red"
- **Setup:** made mostly on an iPhone in GarageBand, singing straight into
  the phone's built-in mic, with guitar and bass through an iRig
  ([Wikipedia, citing DJ Mag and Wired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Lacy%27s_Demo);
  [MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/steve-lacy-garageband-iphone)).
  He held a pop filter up close to the phone's mic. He also made the beat
  for Kendrick Lamar's "Pride" on his phone
  ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/one-of-kendrick-lamars-producers-makes-beats-on-his-iphone-but-he-has-a-problem-with-the-iphone-7/articleshow/58183442.cms)).
- **Lesson:** band-limited phone audio can be the aesthetic. Fit the beat
  around it instead of trying to "restore" it.
- **Atlas:** 036, 115.

## Grimes: *Visions* (2012)
- **Setup:** recorded on GarageBand in her apartment over three weeks
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visions_(Grimes_album))).
  Gear: Roland Juno-G, Line 6 M9 and BOSS VE-20 vocal processors; GarageBand
  only for tracking
  ([Tiny Mix Tapes](https://tinymixtapes.com/node/120072%3Fver%3Damp)).
  Before Halfaxa her earliest work went through the laptop mic
  ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/producers-engineers/grimes-sound-design-is-my-favourite-part-of-the-recording-process)).
- **Lesson:** heavy vocal effects (pedals, reverb, layers) can turn a thin
  source into a sound of its own. Effects are part of the performance.
- **Atlas:** 036, 176, 189.

## Clairo: "Pretty Girl" (2017)
- **Setup:** written and produced in GarageBand with a small keyboard in
  about two hours; the video was filmed on her laptop webcam
  ([Wikipedia](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Pretty_Girl_(Clairo_song))).
  Her early bedroom setup: Yamaha PSR-170, Focusrite Scarlett 2i2, GarageBand
  ([Mixdown](https://mixdownmag.com.au/features/gear-rundown-clairo/index.php?p=frontpage)).
  Certified 2x platinum (RIAA).
- **Lesson:** soft, flat, close delivery is a style. Don't compress or
  brighten it into a "pop vocal".
- **Atlas:** 064, 127.

## Lil Peep: *Crybaby* (2016), *Hellboy* (2016)
- **Setup:** recorded *Crybaby* in three days on a $150 mic with a sock over
  it as a pop filter
  ([Uproxx, citing Ham On Everything](https://uproxx.com/music/lil-peep-crybaby-mixtape-throwback-interview)).
  Started in his bedroom on a MacBook running GarageBand with a Guitar
  Center mic ([Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/feature/lil-peep-tragedy-torment-804686/)).
  *Hellboy* was recorded and mixed by himself in GarageBand
  ([DJBooth/Audiomack](https://djbooth.net/features/2020-09-25-lil-peep-hellboy-remembering-interviews-audiomack/)).
- **Lesson:** a makeshift pop filter dulls highs but stops plosives. Plan
  to add top back with saturation or a shelf. Emotional delivery outranks
  fidelity.
- **Atlas:** 026, 046, 105.

## Fiona Apple: *Fetch the Bolt Cutters* (2020)
- **Setup:** recorded largely at her Venice Beach home, mostly in
  GarageBand. She kept long unedited takes because she didn't know how to
  edit them ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fetch_the_Bolt_Cutters)).
  Household objects as percussion, and an un-soundproofed home studio
  ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/think/opinion/fiona-apple-s-new-album-fetch-bolt-cutters-creative-powerful-ncna1186711)).
  Dogs barking were left in. Grammy winner.
- **Lesson:** room noise and bleed are a choice. Decide whether they're
  character or a defect before removing them.
- **Atlas:** 010, 019, 022.

## Doja Cat: "Mooo!" (2018)
- **Setup:** written and recorded in six hours in her bedroom, from a
  Wes Montgomery sample a producer chopped and sent the night before.
  She built the beat and recorded vocals in Logic Pro, then made the video
  the same day with a green sheet nailed to the wall
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooo!)).
- **Lesson:** speed and personality carried a "throwaway" song.
  Mix-buddy's job is to keep a fast bedroom session clean, not slow.
- **Atlas:** 001, 002, 205.

## Lil Nas X: "Old Town Road" (2018)
- **Setup:** beat leased for $30 from YoungKio, who made it in his bedroom
  in FL Studio by sampling Nine Inch Nails' "34 Ghosts IV"
  ([Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4920422)). Vocals
  recorded in under an hour at CinCoYo in Atlanta on a $20 Tuesday rate
  ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/lil-nas-x-says-he-paid-just-dollar50-to-record-old-town-road)).
  Before this he wrote and recorded songs in his closet
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Nas_X)).
- **Lesson:** a two-track beat plus a quick vocal can carry a hit. The
  mix problem is fitting a vocal onto a finished stereo beat.
- **Atlas:** 011, 205, 192.

## Burial: *Untrue* (2007)
- **Setup:** built in Sound Forge, a waveform editor with no sequencer or
  grid, on an old computer
  ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/im-not-a-musician-i-was-always-scared-of-people-who-had-studios-how-burial-produced-the-21st-centurys-most-influential-electronic-album-on-a-rubbish-dying-computer-with-outdated-software)).
  He avoided sequencers because perfectly timed drums "sound rubbish"
  ([Wikipedia](https://wikimili.com/en/Burial_(musician))).
  Mercury Prize nominee.
- **Lesson:** off-grid timing and heavily pitched or stretched vocal
  samples are a style. Don't "fix" swing that's intended.
- **Atlas:** 162, 171.

## Tame Impala (Kevin Parker): *Currents* (2015)
- **Setup:** written, played, recorded and mixed by Parker at his home
  studio in Fremantle
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currents_(album));
  [AudioTechnology](https://audiotechnology.com/?p=32047)). He deliberately
  sets limits on his vocal recording to get different vocal sounds
  ([Sound On Sound, 2020](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4921408?page=2)).
- **Lesson:** limit the tools on purpose for character, and vary vocal
  treatments between songs and sections.
- **Atlas:** 122, 199.

## Olivia Rodrigo & Dan Nigro: "Drivers License" (2021)
- **Setup:** Nigro wrote and recorded most of *Sour* and *Guts* at Amusement
  Studios, his home studio in East LA
  ([Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-olivia-rodrigo-vampire);
  [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/daniel-nigro-universal-audio-interview)).
  He worked out of a small LA garage before
  ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/?p=9523164)). Some vintage-sounding
  vocals are a fancy mic plus a "vintage" plugin, not old gear
  ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/dan-nigro-olivia-rodrigo-sour)).
- **Lesson:** lo-fi colour can be added in the mix, so record clean and
  degrade on purpose.
- **Atlas:** 115, 120.

## Charlie Puth: *Voicenotes* (2018)
- **Setup:** produced and recorded in a room of his house. Song ideas
  started as voice memos on his phone, hence the title
  ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/609061081)).
- **Not supported by sources:** the SM7B, hotel rooms and specific EQ
  chain in earlier notes. Removed.
- **Atlas:** 049.
